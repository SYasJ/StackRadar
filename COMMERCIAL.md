# Commercial license

StackRadar is **free for personal, non-commercial use** under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). **Using it for commercial purposes needs a paid commercial license.**

## Free: no license needed

- Personal use on your own computer: side projects, learning, hobby work, open-source projects you maintain for free
- Students, teachers and academic research
- Charities, schools, public-research bodies, public-safety and government institutions
  (the PolyForm Noncommercial license explicitly covers these, whatever their funding source)
- Trying StackRadar out to decide whether your company wants it (a **30-day evaluation**)

## Paid: needs a commercial license

- Using StackRadar at or for a company, on work or client projects, or as part of a paid service
- Shipping, bundling, reselling or hosting StackRadar (or a modified version) as part of a commercial product
- Using StackRadar's code in a commercial product

Not sure which applies? Ask. Small teams and solo freelancers usually get simple terms.

## How to get one

Open an issue titled **"Commercial license"** at
<https://github.com/SYasJ/StackRadar/issues/new?title=Commercial%20license> with your company name and how many people
would use it, or contact [@SYasJ](https://github.com/SYasJ) through GitHub. You'll get a quote and a written license.
Pricing is per seat per year.

## Third-party components

The desktop app bundles open-source components (Electron, electron-updater, the Python runtime) under their own
licenses (MIT, PSF and others). Their licenses are unchanged and ship inside the app.
